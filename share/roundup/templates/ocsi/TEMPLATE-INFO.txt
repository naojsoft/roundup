Name: ocsi
Description: Subaru Telescope OCS issue tracker (OCSI). Extends the classic
             template with a category/group permission model: issues are
             partitioned into categories, and a user's visibility and edit
             rights on individual issue properties are derived from their
             group memberships via permission bundles. Also adds rank,
             importance, resolution and site classifications.
Intended-For: Subaru Telescope SSD
